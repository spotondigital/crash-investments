ABOUT US

Our Background

Crash Investments (Pty) Ltd, trading as MySystems, is a proudly South African, 100% black-owned SMME incorporated in 2018. Over the past six years, the organisation has evolved from a lean start-up into a multi-disciplinary procurement, supply, and logistics group serving both public-sector entities and private corporations across Gauteng and beyond.

Our strategic intent is to build a diversified group of businesses that operates across multiple industry verticals — positioning specialist subsidiary capabilities wherever demand exists, while maintaining the agility and personal accountability of an owner-managed enterprise. We are intentional about growing through skills transfer, supplier development, and the progressive formalisation of each business unit as it matures.

Our principal has over 18 years of cross-industry experience spanning ICT hardware supply, software, network infrastructure, electrical components, facilities management, driver instruction, décor hire, catering, printing, and learning material development. This breadth allows Crash Investments to engage credibly across sectors, identify procurement needs early, and execute with precision.

VISION

To be a leading South African supply and solutions group — recognised for reliability, transformation, and innovation across every industry we serve.

MISSION

To source, supply, and deliver quality goods and services efficiently — while creating employment, developing skills, and advancing emerging black-owned enterprises.

VALUES

Integrity   |   Accountability   |   Quality   |   Transformation   |   Innovation
